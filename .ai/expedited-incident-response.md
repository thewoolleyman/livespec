# Expedited incident response

This guidance realizes core `SPECIFICATION/contracts.md` §"Expedited incident
response policy". The released shared execution interfaces in
`livespec-dev-tooling` own mode resolution and flag parsing. Do not copy a
parser into a hook, template, operator command or Driver.

## Establish eligibility before selecting work

An authorized repair uses a local secondary worktree and the existing incident
work item; it needs neither a factory run nor a green-master precondition.
That exception exists only after this repository has recorded capability proof:
its normally installed hook and CI readers, provisioned labels and event
subscriptions, an independently available known-good released observer, and a
recovery principal authorized against every overlapping protection rule.
Instruction-only adoption is not capability proof. Without proof, both flags
are inert and the full normal path remains in effect.

Use the shared resolver before prechecks, autofix, pairing, cache reads,
delegation, replay or fallbacks. It resolves trusted event, incident item,
attempt, phase, repository and exact base/head from the receipt. A marker or
ambient flag alone is insufficient. EXPEDITED_INCIDENT_RESPONSE selects the
receipt-bound repair; EXPEDITED_INCIDENT_RESPONSE_REVERT takes precedence for
the recorded emergency inverse. Both are invocation-scoped and must never
become host, repository or image defaults. Trusted normal master and normal
factory follow-up clear both before interpreting inherited values, even
malformed values; ordinary malformed configuration receives a named refusal.

Neither flag supplies credentials, workflow-edit permission or host-mutation
authority. Keep the normal hooks installed and call their supported shared
interfaces. Do not use `--no-verify`, disable hooks, evade tool refusals or
weaken ordinary branch protection. Changes to this policy or its implementing
tooling must themselves use the normal development and rollout policy.

## Collect honest focused repair evidence

Run applicable local gates that finish in less than sixty seconds. Defer known
slow or unclassified work explicitly before launch, including cluster probes
and delegated work. If a selected unknown gate reaches sixty seconds,
terminate its owned children and record deferred-timeout. Do not split a slow
suite into nominally fast wrappers or restore the normal aggregate through a
fallback. Retain real fast-check failures and correct failing assertions.

Record changed or directly related tests, existing coverage thresholds scoped
to the cumulative touched executable files, and one manual reproduction with
expected and actual outcomes. Give missing evidence an explicit operator risk
disposition. Place the exact legal adjacent code or test comment
`EXPEDITED_INCIDENT_RESPONSE flag: <incident work-item id>` beside the repair;
provide a valid comment carrier for deletion-only or non-commentable changes.
Keep the structured PR receipt bound to this exact PR/head, with retained
checks, deferred work and focused evidence. Record deferred replay honestly;
never fabricate normal TDD trailers, full-pass tokens or factory proof.

The receipt-selected repair PR retains declared fast CI checks and discloses
omitted coverage and specification enforcement in its stable required
aggregate. A new head or mode invalidates earlier verdicts. Deferred checks
are not executed passes. Every master push still runs the complete normal
job graph with both flags disabled.

## Recover immediately and repay on the same item

Before merge, retain a durable recovery receipt and the independently serving
known-good observer. The first failing required master leaf job triggers
immediate recovery: do not wait for the aggregate, failure classification,
reruns, a fix-forward claim, factory work, local quality gates or revert-PR CI.
Use only the independently provisioned recovery principal and recorded action.
Validate the complete inverse against current master and its expected head,
preserve later unrelated edits, and record overlap conflicts rather than
overwriting them. Keep operation identity and generation fences across
restarts and reconcile receipts after ledger, wrapper or runner outages.

Let the original master run finish. Classify its failures as incident-caused,
pre-existing, infrastructure or unresolved; retain failed, skipped, cancelled
and never-created required work and own missing original-tree checks as debt.
A corrected attempt keeps the incident identity and supplies new correction
or measured resolution evidence. Never reland unchanged incident code merely
to retry a pre-existing broken base. Fence bad-artifact adoption, supersede
pending pin carriers and own corrective rollout for already affected
consumers. Irreversible external mutations need a recorded safe compensating
operation before unattended rollback is eligible.

Successful normal master CI starts an unexpedited factory follow-up on the
same incident work item. Verify the cumulative original change under full
normal gates, remove the marker, and repay evidence and specification debt.
A deferred-proof defect alone is fixed there without reverting healthy master.
An attributable regression on master, another PR or an adopted consumer
triggers recovery and fences stale cleanup, even after closure; retain the
failing job/test and connecting change evidence. An unrelated flake is not
attribution. This requirement never delays first-leaf failure recovery.

Keep the incident open until the final normal factory PR and subsequent normal
master CI succeed, the marker is removed and debt is repaid. A required
workflow correction uses an authorized host publisher with a carrier-specific
receipt to publish the normally validated factory tree to the same carrier PR;
the factory dispatch credential keeps its workflow-write restriction. Track
each fleet surface as implemented, deliberately-normal or not-applicable and
prove its serving version before enablement.
