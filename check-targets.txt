# bound), or hit the cap, is `slow` and is deferred on the expedited path. A gate that encodes a normal-path precondition an incident
# commit cannot meet by design is `not-applicable` and names that precondition.

# re-timed alone at lower load, and the faster of the two runs classifies it,
# because `slow` defers the gate and a fast gate that overruns 60 s is still
# recorded as a deferred timeout: the faster reading is the safer one.
# 85 are `fast`, 4 `slow` and 3 `not-applicable`. The expedited hooks
