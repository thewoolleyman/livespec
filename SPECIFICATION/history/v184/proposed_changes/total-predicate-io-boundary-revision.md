## Decision and Rationale

RATIFIED UNDER AN EXPLICIT MAINTAINER ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF A CONTRADICTION FOR WHICH NO DESIGN RECORD SURVIVES. A prior revise pass declined to process this proposal and left it pending, because by its own text it resolves a conflict between two ratified statements — condition 1 of the rendering-boundary clause convicts a function whose only direct primitive is a total predicate, while member 1's uninhabited-track rationale forbids converting it — and spec.md section 'Intent preservation and design-record authority' holds that when no design record is cited or reachable for the conflicting statements, that absence is itself a maintainer finding and the conflict MUST NOT be self-resolved in either direction. That gate was discharged by the maintainer, not by this agent: the absence was surfaced with its measurement, and the maintainer ruled explicitly to acknowledge the contradiction in this record rather than to cite a design record for the two clauses. THE ALTERNATIVE WAS CONSIDERED AND REFUSED ON THE MERITS: no surviving record establishes the original intent of condition 1 or member 1, so citing one would manufacture provenance for a decision nobody can reconstruct — an authoritative statement not backed by the evidence it implies, which is precisely the defect class this rule set exists to remove. A true record of an unrecoverable intent is strictly better than a false record of a recovered one. ON THE MERITS the proposal stands as filed. The premise is measured against CPython 3.10 with both polarities from one probe, so the negative result is credible rather than blind. The framing is correct and is the part most likely to be argued: this is a CORRECTION to the definition of 'I/O boundary', NOT a fifth member of the exemption set, which remains EXHAUSTIVE, and it moves in BOTH directions — relaxing for total-predicate-only functions and TIGHTENING for open, readlink, chown, truncate and directory walks, which genuinely raise and are missed today. A rule that only relaxed would be a relief deserving a relief's scrutiny; the tightening half is what makes it a fidelity fix. It proposes NO enumerated verb list as spec text, since a list is what failed in both directions and would need re-ratifying every standard-library revision, while binding the implementation to decide failability mechanically, store no claim, record per-verb determinations with evidence, resolve unresolved ambiguity as FAILABLE so doubt TIGHTENS, and explicitly refuse the one-directional implementation that drops total predicates from a list and stops there.
